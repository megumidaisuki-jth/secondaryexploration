# Result-blind Results and source-data reporting contract

## Freeze timing and argument

This contract was frozen on 2026-08-11 while formal execution contained 168
complete blocks and six additional singleton workers were active. No formal
endpoint value, contrast, interval, gate decision or direction was read to
choose the presentation.

One-sentence Results argument:

> In registered synthetic hypergraph payment networks, the Results will report
> whether resource-matched service contrasts reproduce across two independent
> phases, using every registered multiplicity-adjusted contrast and bounded
> descriptive evidence without phase pooling or mechanism claims.

The independent unit is one parent graph within one node-count and parent-model
stratum. Each contrast has 20 parents in each of three named strata, hence
`n=60` independent parents per phase. The seven held-out traffic traces within
a parent are nested measurements and never increase `n`.

## Trust gate

No Results prose, table or figure may be populated until all of the following
strict artifacts exist and pass complete source replay:

1. the formal 240-block run summary, finalization witness and phase evidence;
2. the confirmation 240-block run summary, finalization witness and phase
   evidence;
3. the 40-record cross-phase replication evidence;
4. both result-blind descriptive topology/balance/cost projections;
5. the execution, orchestration, analysis and projection revision witnesses.

The 2026 Lightning panel remains outside this gate. It can enter only a
separately labelled structural diagnostic after lawful exact preservation and
never enters the synthetic bootstrap or a replication state.

## Mandatory complete reporting

### Machine-readable source data

The canonical source-data export must contain every one of the following,
without a result-dependent omission rule:

- all 40 registered contrasts in each phase, including exact estimate, lower
  and upper interval endpoints, registered beneficial direction, tier, gate
  state, phase status, parent count and three stratum counts;
- all 40 cross-phase records, including both interval directions, both gate
  states, both phase-success flags, point-sign agreement and replication state;
- all parent-model values required to replay the 40 estimates;
- all event-coverage cells for `q=0.10`, while retaining
  `quantile_estimate=null`;
- every demand-aware changed/unchanged activity cell, including empty or
  singleton strata;
- all 13 descriptive metrics for four source families, four sizes, three
  parent-model strata and both phases;
- phase runtime, block count, execution environment and source fingerprints;
- explicit source-data schema and content fingerprint.

Exact fractions remain authoritative. Display values use three decimal places
with leading zeros; a displayed zero is written `0.000`, never `<0.001`.
Rounding does not determine direction, gate or replication state.

No p value is derived from a percentile endpoint. Within each phase, the
registered analysis is reported as a studywise 95% family of 40 contrasts,
arranged as eight local five-contrast hierarchies,
local confidence parameter `159/160`, 20,000 bootstrap resamples and two-sided
bounds using per-tail adjusted empirical probability `1/1600`. The manuscript
calls the plotted bounds **multiplicity-adjusted percentile interval bounds**
rather than assigning them an invented marginal confidence percentage.

### Main text and display items

The main display set is fixed before results:

1. **Figure 1 — design and evidence flow.** Result-free schematic from parent
   strata through paired held-out traces, phase evidence and independent
   confirmation.
2. **Figure 2 — all eight global contrasts.** Two endpoint panels, four size
   rows each, with formal and confirmation estimates and adjusted interval
   bounds. Zero and the registered beneficial direction are labelled. No row
   may be omitted because of width, sign or replication state.
3. **Figure 3 — complete 8 by 5 replication matrix.** Every global and named
   contrast appears once. Cell fill encodes only the enumerated replication
   state; paired formal/confirmation glyphs encode each interval direction;
   and a fixed border or hatch distinguishes a closed secondary gate from an
   open but inconclusive test. Stars and significance-only labels are
   prohibited.
4. **Table 1 — global numerical results.** The eight global rows report both
   phases, `n=60` with `20+20+20` strata, adjusted bounds, interval directions
   and cross-phase state.

The complete 40-row numerical registry, parent-model summaries, event coverage,
activity sensitivity and 13-metric descriptive projection go to linked source
data or supplementary tables. Main-text prose may summarize the complete
registry but cannot hide a discordant, harmful, gate-closed or inconclusive
record.

## Frozen paragraph architecture

The Results section has eight jobs in this order:

1. establish artifact completeness, phase independence and `n`;
2. report all eight equal-family global contrasts, led by cross-phase states;
3. report all 32 named-family contrasts under their phase-specific global
   gates;
4. describe compatibility or heterogeneity across ER-GNM, BA and fixed-count
   SBM parent strata without treating strata as extra tests;
5. report `q=0.10` event coverage only, never a numerical censored quantile;
6. report demand-aware changed/unchanged activity as descriptive sensitivity;
7. report all 13 topology/balance/cost descriptors as exploratory consistency
   evidence without causal or mediation language;
8. report runtime and the external-validity boundary, including the separately
   gated Lightning diagnostic.

Observation and interpretation remain separate. Results paragraphs state what
was observed; causal, mechanistic and practical interpretation belongs in the
Discussion and remains bounded by the registered claim map.

These eight jobs may be combined into four concise subsections but not omitted
or reordered. Together with the three compact main figures and Table 1, the
Results target the frozen 2.6 printed-page allocation in
`manuscript/tnsm-submission-contract.md`. The complete 40-row registry,
coverage, activity and 13-metric records remain in Source Data rather than
being repeated in prose. If a rendered draft exceeds the allocation, it enters
the existing page-overrun decision gate; no row or adverse state may be removed
to recover space.

## Claim and wording state machine

- `independently confirmed` is permitted only when both phase-success flags are
  true. For a named family, this necessarily requires its adjusted interval to
  be beneficial and its corresponding global gate open in each phase.
- two harmful adjusted intervals are reported as a repeated harmful direction,
  not as `no difference`;
- `formal_only` and `confirmation_only` name the successful phase and explicitly
  state that independent confirmation was not met;
- `neither` is expanded using the two interval directions and gate states. It
  never implies equivalence;
- point-sign agreement is descriptive and cannot override adjusted intervals;
- parent-model directional compatibility is descriptive, not robustness;
- descriptor alignment is `consistent with`, never `caused by`, `mediated by`
  or `explains`.

## Figure and table statistics

Every quantitative legend or caption must state:

- what each point and bound represents;
- that `n=60` parent graphs per contrast and phase, stratified `20+20+20`;
- that traffic traces are nested within parents;
- endpoint units and registered beneficial direction;
- 20,000 parent-stratified bootstrap resamples and adjusted probability
  `1/1600` in each tail;
- that the 95% family applies separately to the 40 contrasts in each phase,
  not jointly to 80 contrasts across both phases;
- that formal and confirmation phases are not pooled;
- where exact fractions and the complete 40-row registry are available.

No bar chart, undefined error bar, star code, `ns`, or selective best-family
highlight is allowed. Parent values may be shown as points only when the plot
preserves the three model strata and does not imply 60 exchangeable pooled
parents.

## Failure conditions

Reporting fails closed if any registered row, phase, stratum count, gate,
direction, source fingerprint or descriptive metric is missing, duplicated,
reordered or inconsistent with strict evidence. A figure or table produced
from manually copied values is not publication evidence.
